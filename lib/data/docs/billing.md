# Billing cycles and duplicate charges

Invoices are generated on the first of each month and charged to the card
on file within 24 hours. If you see two charges in the same billing period,
it's usually one of:

- A plan upgrade mid-cycle (prorated charge) plus the regular monthly charge
- A retried payment after a card was briefly declined, where both the
  failed and the retried attempt still show as pending on your bank
  statement for a few days
- A genuine duplicate charge from a billing system error

Support can confirm which of these applies by pulling the account's
invoice history, and can issue a refund directly for a genuine duplicate.
