# Refund policy

Support can issue a full refund without manager approval when:

- The charge is a confirmed duplicate, or
- The customer cancelled within 7 days of being charged, or
- A service outage materially affected the billing period

Refunds are issued back to the original payment method and typically post
within 5–10 business days. Always open a support ticket alongside a refund
so there's a record tied to the account, even if the refund itself is
processed immediately.

Refunds larger than the account's most recent invoice require escalation
to a billing lead — do not self-approve those.
