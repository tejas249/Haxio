# Why accounts get suspended

An account is suspended when an invoice goes unpaid for more than 14 days
past its due date. Suspension blocks API access and dashboard logins for
every teammate on the account, but does not delete any data.

To resolve a suspension:

1. Pay the outstanding invoice from **Settings > Billing**.
2. Access is restored automatically within a few minutes of payment
   clearing — no need to contact support for this part.
3. If the invoice amount looks wrong, open a support ticket before paying
   so it can be reviewed first.

Enterprise plan accounts get a 7-day grace period reminder email before
suspension; Starter and Pro plans do not.
